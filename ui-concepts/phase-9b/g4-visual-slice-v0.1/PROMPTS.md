Production-ready ultra-wide floor/map mural: left woodland trail and camp, center stone

## backdrop_shop

Production-ready 16:9 traveling merchant workshop inside the spire: lamplit caravan tent,
old forge, shelves, maps, relic silhouettes, herbs, teal canopy and brass fittings. Keep
environmental detail at far edges and upper third, with a broad dark central counter/table

## event_tranquil_grove

Production event illustration “Tranquil Grove”: a secret circular grove beneath a broken
spire dome, ancient pale tree around a glowing spring, bronze bells and faded ribbons,
moss-covered knight statues, and one kneeling traveler seen from behind. Quiet, uncanny and
hopeful; 4:3 composition, clear focal point and darker outer edges; no choices or UI frame.

## backdrop_battle

Production-ready 16:9 auto-battler arena: ruined circular forge-temple with two horizontal
staging terraces. The upper enemy terrace uses cold iron and muted crimson; the lower player
terrace uses old brass, deep teal and faint runes. Preserve two flat uncluttered five-unit
rows separated by a central gap; no characters, cards, slot numbers, text or UI.
