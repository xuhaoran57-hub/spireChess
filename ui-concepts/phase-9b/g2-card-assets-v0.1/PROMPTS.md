## 共同生产约束

Create a brand-new production-direction game asset in the warm hand-painted
Wandering Storybook style: watercolor and dark walnut/colored ink on fibrous
ivory paper, visible paper grain, irregular brush edges, broad readable
shapes, restrained detail, and a warm adventurous mood. Keep one dominant
subject and a clear silhouette at small UI size. Background must stay one or
two contrast steps below the subject. Do not copy a reference character,
pose, face, composition, or unique prop.

For portrait card artwork use a vertical 2:3 composition and keep critical
details out of the outer 7%. For relics and the diagnostic asset use a square,
centered icon composition. Artwork only: no card frame, UI, labels, title
ribbon, letters, numbers, readable writing, logo, watermark, signature, or
brand mark. Avoid photorealism, glossy digital collectible-card rendering,
anime/gacha styling, and childish mascot art.

## Token

### 1. 幼灵 / `token_young_spirit`

Create a small faceless forest seed spirit standing beneath an ancient tree.
Its body is a compact pear-shaped seed pod wrapped in overlapping bark scales,
with exactly two large leaf ears, thin twig arms, and short root feet. A single
warm amber seed light glows through a narrow opening in its chest. The
silhouette should feel young and fragile without becoming a cute humanlike
mascot. Quiet moss-and-bark forest, muted green and brown palette, subject
centered and immediately readable. No eyes, mouth, human face, clothing,
weapons, extra creatures, flowers, text, or UI.

### 2. 双尾狐影 / `token_two_tailed_fox_shadow`

Create one semi-realistic shadow fox crouched low in a moonlit forest. Its
body is deep blue-gray with restrained leaflike fur edges. Show exactly two
large, unmistakably separate tails; both tails rise behind the fox and curve
into a protective arch while remaining individually traceable from base to
tip. Place exactly two tiny warm leaf lights far behind the subject as a quiet
echo motif. Keep the fox alert and uncanny rather than cute or aggressive.
No third tail, merged extra tail, additional animal, visible magic glyph,
shrine imagery, anime kitsune styling, neon effects, text, or UI.

Final controlled edit: preserve the selected fox, palette, pose, paper texture,
and forest; redraw only the rear silhouette so there are exactly two clearly
distinguishable giant tails, and retain exactly two warm background lights.

### 3. 迅捷幼灵 / `token_swift_young_spirit`

Create the same species language as the young spirit but as a distinct swift
variant: one faceless pear-shaped seed-pod spirit sprinting along a forest
path, exactly two leaf ears swept backward, thin twig arms balancing the run,
and two long root legs in a clear airborne stride. A warm amber seed core
glows in the chest. A restrained trail of loose leaves conveys speed without
particle clutter. Bright, airy green woodland with a readable dark subject.
No face, eyes, mouth, humanoid child anatomy, shoes, weapon, extra creature,
motion text, icon, or UI.

Final controlled edit: preserve the selected running composition and palette,
but replace any mascotlike or ambiguous body with a faceless pear-shaped bark
seed pod, two leaf ears, twig arms, root legs, and one warm seed core.

## 法术

### 4. 小型锻体 / `minor_tempering`

Create a practical caravan repair scene centered on a seated wooden-and-iron
training puppet on a workbench. A compact screw clamp presses one new dark
iron shoulder plate into place; a restrained orange heat seam at the opposite
joint suggests tempering and stat growth. Include only a few rivets and simple
hand tools as secondary details. The puppet is an inanimate workshop dummy,
not a human and not a living robot. Warm dusty camp workshop, broad wood and
iron shapes. No blacksmith character, face, five-fingered hands, sword,
steampunk mechanisms, spark shower, readable plans, text, or UI.

### 5. 免费刷新 / `free_refresh`

Create a traveling-market display built around one large circular wooden
turntable visibly rotating. Arrange a restrained selection of merchant goods
on it: bottles, rolled cloth, a small brass viewing instrument, a pouch, and a
plain case. At the central spindle, exactly two long blank parchment tags lift
in the breeze to suggest replacement choices. In the foreground, one untouched
coin rests alone in a shallow dish, clearly separate from the rotating stock,
to communicate a free refresh. Warm open-air caravan market. No merchant
character, readable price, letters, numbers, card shapes, duplicated coin,
glowing UI arrows, text, or interface.

### 6. 高阶发现 / `advanced_discovery`

Create a tall fold-out traveling viewing case opened vertically on a twilight
observatory-market table. The case has exactly three large viewing apertures,
stacked as three distinct choices: an upper circular brass lens with a distant
winged mountain silhouette, a middle parchment-toned window with a stag
silhouette, and a lower parchment-toned window with a wolf silhouette. The
three openings are the dominant readable structure; use indigo night,
parchment, leather, and restrained brass. Include only sparse observatory
shapes in the background. No fourth aperture, labels, readable constellations,
cards, buttons, icons, selection arrows, text, or UI.

### 7. 战前赐福 / `prebattle_benediction`

Create a prebattle camp scene dominated by one patched indigo-and-russet
banner canopy stretched between rough wooden poles. Beneath it stand exactly
three small, dark adventurer silhouettes facing outward together. A single
broad translucent pale-cyan protective dome encloses the group, while a few
fresh green shoots rise at their feet to suggest defense plus growth. Warm
dawn breaks through a restrained caravan battlefield background. Keep faces
and individual equipment indistinct. No fourth hero, clergy figure, readable
crest, holy text, stat icons, runes, spell circle, UI, or title.

## 遗珍

### 8. 回魂丧钟 / `crown_echo_bell`

Create a square relic icon of one aged bronze memorial bell hanging from a
compact dark wooden frame. The top crossbar carries a simple five-point crown
silhouette and a small plain leaf-shaped medallion, with no heraldic text.
The bell has a leaf charm tied beneath its clapper. Exactly two pale,
concentric painted sound rings pass behind the bell, suggesting one life
returning as an echo. Centered object, ivory paper background, strong
silhouette, weathered bronze and walnut. No person, skull, funeral lettering,
readable emblem, floating soul face, UI border, text, or watermark.

### 9. 千盾王冠 / `crown_thousand_shields`

Create a square relic icon of a heavy crown constructed entirely from many
overlapping blackened-iron heater shields. The shield points create the crown
peaks; two larger front shields carry only a restrained pale-cyan rim glow.
Use worn charcoal metal, simple brass edging and rivets, with a broad,
symmetrical silhouette on ivory paper. It must read first as one crown and
second as accumulated armor. No head or wearer, gemstone jewelry, readable
coat of arms, weapons, castle, text, UI frame, or watermark.

### 10. 漏刻齿轮 / `curio_refresh_gear`

Create a square relic icon of a compact portable merchant refresh mechanism
on a worn wooden base. A simple wood-and-brass frame holds one large central
brass gear; above it, a small ceramic drip cup releases exactly one blue-gray
water drop. Below the gear, one copper coin rests in a shallow brass tray.
A wrapped carry handle and one pull cord make the object feel practical and
travel-worn. Keep the mechanism simple, handcrafted, and readable rather than
steampunk. No clock face, numerals, readable markings, extra coins, dense
gears, electricity, character, text, UI frame, or watermark.

## 诊断图

### 11. 缺图诊断 / `fallback_missing_art`

Create a square, intentionally unmistakable missing-art diagnostic texture,
not a polished fantasy illustration. Fill the background with a high-contrast
black and vivid magenta checkerboard. Center a large torn ivory-paper
broken-image pictogram: a flat yellow sun above two flat black mountain
triangles, with one thick jagged black crack splitting the paper vertically.
Use hard, simple shapes that remain legible at thumbnail size. No words,
letters, numbers, logos, watermark, character, decorative frame, or subtle
low-contrast treatment. This asset must look obviously wrong when it appears
in production UI.
