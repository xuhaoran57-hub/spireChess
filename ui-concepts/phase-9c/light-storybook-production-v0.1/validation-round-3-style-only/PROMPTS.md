## 卡面共同块

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: standalone landscape game-card illustration master,
approximately 5:4, full-bleed art without a card frame.

Image 1 is the ONLY reference. Use it solely for the frozen Light Wandering
Storybook visual language: airy hand-painted watercolor and colored ink on
warm ivory paper, visible fibrous grain, irregular brush edges, loose
walnut-and-colored linework and the reference palette. Do not copy its collage
layout, sample objects, lettering or captions.

Create one entirely new, unnamed species exemplar. Use only the broad species
premise supplied below. Do not use or reinterpret any existing minion, named
role, ability, pose, equipment set, object-count rule or prior character
silhouette. Do not impose a universal body plan. Invent a fresh anatomy and
silhouette freely from the species premise.

Exactly one main being; broad thumbnail-readable silhouette; whole subject and
important details inside the central 80%; outer 7% free of essential
information; generous breathing room; no crop. This is one illustration, not
a style sheet.

Diffuse morning-to-afternoon daylight is the main illumination. Pale paper and
sky remain visible; shadows are open, soft and chromatic. At least half of the
image reads as light or middle-light, with only tiny deep accents.

No card border, UI, title strip, readable text, letters, numbers, logo,
signature or watermark; no duplicated subject; no photorealism, glossy 3D,
anime-card rendering or heavy opaque digital paint.
```

Scene: an open sunlit craft yard belonging to a traveling workshop, suggested

supports the species but does not dictate a character mechanic.
```

environment supports the species but does not dictate a character mechanic.
```

### 星契

```text
Species premise: a living traveler shaped by skyward curiosity, celestial
navigation, fellowship and distant journeys, expressed through indigo,
paper-white, soft violet, light open arcs, fine woven ribbons and modest aged

Show a marvelous tall traveling tower mounted on a broad many-wheeled caravan

and menu controls.
```

### 商店

```text

canvas and teal cloth on a sunny overlook. Arrange a restrained variety of
travel goods around the perimeter. Preserve a broad pale low-detail central

or customers.
```
