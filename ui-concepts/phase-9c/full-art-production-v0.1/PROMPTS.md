## 明亮风格生产覆盖规则

以 `../../phase-9b/style-tiles/style-tile-d-wandering-storybook-v0.1.png`
作为风格、色板、光照和气质的唯一参考。使用晨间至下午的漫射日光和彩色开放
阴影；卡面至少 50% 为亮部或中亮部，近黑面积不超过约 12%。背景以简化形状和
降低细节保持安静，不得整体降曝光；炉火仅作局部强调，不得作为主光。

铸魂只冻结“由炉火灵性驱动的铸造生命”及其锻造、盾甲、修复和誓约文化，不要求
三爪、空心躯壳、断开甲片、炉栅或楔形脚等统一身体结构。

## 校准项

### 炉心火种 / `hearth_core_spark`

```text
One small tier-one Forge Soul, a forged living being animated by furnace
spirit. Its compact hand-worked iron body protects one warm orange hearth core.
One protective plate has just split and fallen away; the exposed core flares
into a single strong forward-pointing ember plume, suggesting “when shield is
lost, gain attack.” Use an original coherent body structure suited to its
low-tier role without mandatory clamps, hollow anatomy or disconnected parts.
Place it in a clear open caravan forge in diffuse afternoon daylight, with
open blue-gray and warm-brown shadows. At least 50% remains light or
middle-light and near-black stays below roughly 12%; ember is a local accent,
not primary light. Horizontal 5:4 card-art composition; one subject inside the
central 80%.
```
